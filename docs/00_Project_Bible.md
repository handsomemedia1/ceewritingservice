# Project Bible - Vision & Strategy

## 1. Project Overview
Cee Writing Hub is a research, education, scholarship, and professional development platform designed primarily for Nigerian students and researchers, scaling globally.

## 2. Core Hubs
*   **Navigation Hub:** The Homepage. Built for discoverability.
*   **Knowledge Hub:** The learning ecosystem (replacing the traditional blog). Follows the Knowledge Pyramid structure (Foundational -> Applied -> Expert).
*   **Services Hub:** Professional services (writing, data analysis).
*   **Tools Hub:** Plagiarism checkers, GPA calculators, scholarship checkers.
*   **Scholarship Hub:** Aggregation and guidance for international scholarships.
*   **Research Repository:** A searchable database of research abstracts, theses, and papers.

## 3. Brand Identity
*   **Vibe:** Premium, modern, authoritative, academic.
*   **Anti-Template:** No generic AI layouts, no decorative stock icons, no excessive card grids without purpose.
*   **Typography:** Playfair Display (headings) & Inter (body).
*   **Palette:** Deep Navy (`#0B1F3A`), Gold (`#C9933A`), Cream (`#FDFAF5`).

## 4. SEO & AI Discoverability
*   SEO is the primary growth engine (Spec 009).
*   Focus on Topical Authority (Pillar pages + cluster content).
*   Semantic HTML, JSON-LD schema on every entity, sub-second TTFB.
