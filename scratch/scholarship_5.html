The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Doctoral Scholarship is one of Canada’s most prestigious and highly coveted awards for doctoral students in the humanities and social sciences. Designed to empower engaged leaders, the scholarship provides generous financial support and integrates scholars into an exceptional multidisciplinary network of mentors, fellows, and alumni. If your research tackles critical societal issues, this opportunity can accelerate both your academic career and your public impact.

Current 2027 Application Status

The application cycle for the 2027 cohort is currently open. Prospective applicants must meet the initial eligibility verification deadline of October 2, 2026, to proceed to the full application stage.

Funding Details

According to the Foundation, the scholarship provides a comprehensive funding package for up to three years, totaling up to $210,000. It is critical to note how this funding is distributed:

  • Stipend: Up to $50,000 per year to cover tuition fees and living expenses. The Foundation guarantees a minimum annual stipend; however, the exact amount may be adjusted if the scholar holds other major external funding.
  • Research and Travel Allowance: Up to $20,000 per year specifically allocated for research, fieldwork, networking, and travel related to the doctoral project and Foundation-led leadership events.
  • Leadership Program: Scholars gain exclusive access to a three-year leadership program designed to nurture their skills in public engagement and translating research into action.

Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for the 2027 scholarship cycle, applicants must meet specific criteria:

  • Academic Standing: You must be in your first or second year of a full-time doctoral program.
  • Citizenship and Affiliation:
    • International students and Canadian Permanent Residents must be enrolled in a full-time doctoral program at a Canadian university.
    • Canadian citizens can be enrolled at a Canadian university or a foreign university.
  • Field of Study: Your research must be within the humanities or social sciences.
  • Thematic Alignment: Your project must align with at least one of the Foundation’s four core themes: Human Rights and Dignity, Responsible Citizenship, Canada and the World, or People and their Natural Environment.

Application Checklist & Important Deadlines

CEE recommends marking these exact dates in your calendar. Missing the initial eligibility step will permanently disqualify you from the 2027 cycle.

  • October 2, 2026: Submit the mandatory eligibility verification form.
  • October 5 – October 12, 2026: Attend the mandatory information sessions (for verified eligible candidates).
  • October 30, 2026 (9:00 a.m. ET): Deadline for your three referees to submit their recommendation letters. CEE advises reminding your referees at least two weeks before this date.
  • November 6, 2026 (4:00 p.m. ET): Final deadline to submit the complete online application package, including transcripts and research proposals.
