The NVIDIA Graduate Fellowship Program provides funding to PhD students who are researching topics that will lead to major advances in the graphics, AI, and high-performance computing industries. As one of the most prestigious industry fellowships available to computing and artificial intelligence researchers, it offers exceptional financial support and direct integration with NVIDIA's cutting-edge research teams.

Last verified / updated: October 2026

Current 2027 Application Status

The application period for the 2027–2028 academic year is currently open. Applications must be submitted by October 30, 2026. Late submissions will not be considered. Given the rigorous selection process and the need for recommendation letters, CEE recommends completing your application materials well ahead of this deadline.

Funding Details

According to the official NVIDIA fellowship guidelines, recipients receive an award of up to $60,000 to support their PhD research. It is important to note how this funding is structured:

  • Grant Amount: Up to $60,000 for the nine-month academic year (2027-2028). This can generally be used to cover living expenses, tuition, or research costs.
  • Internship: A mandatory paid in-person summer internship at an NVIDIA research office prior to the fellowship year (Summer 2027). This provides both additional income and invaluable industry experience.

CEE advises applicants to consult their university's financial aid or graduate studies office, as some institutions may have specific policies on how external grants like this interact with existing graduate funding packages or stipends.

Eligibility Requirements

According to the official program rules, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Must have completed at least the first year of PhD-level studies at the time of application.
  • Must be a full-time, active PhD student throughout the nine-month award year.
  • Must be engaged in active research related to areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, robotics, autonomous vehicles, computer graphics, healthcare, or high-performance computing.
  • Must be available for the mandatory summer 2027 internship at NVIDIA.

Application Checklist

Ensure you have prepared the following documents for your submission. If you need assistance structuring your proposal or refining your CV to meet industry standards, consider utilizing CEE Writing Services to help you present your research compellingly.

  • Research Proposal: Maximum 2 pages (plus bibliography). This should clearly outline your research goals, methodology, and how it aligns with NVIDIA's current research interests.
  • Resume or Curriculum Vitae (CV): Highlighting your academic achievements, publications, and relevant technical experience.
  • Recommendation Letters: 2 to 3 letters of recommendation are required. Crucially, one of these letters must be from your official thesis advisor. Ensure you request these early through the NVIDIA research portal.
