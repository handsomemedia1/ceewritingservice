Understanding Funding at Carleton University

Funding is a critical component of your PhD application. According to the university, when you apply for admission, you are automatically considered for funding; there is no separate application required for standard graduate funding packages. This standard package typically includes:

  • Teaching Assistantships (TAs): Paid positions assisting with undergraduate courses.
  • Research Assistantships (RAs): Paid research work typically funded by your supervisor's research grants.
  • Scholarships and Bursaries: Merit-based internal awards provided by the university or department.

CEE advises that while standard packages cover significant costs, you should carefully review the specific funding offer upon admission, as living expenses in Ottawa and international tuition fees will need to be met. Additionally, CEE recommends applying for external funding (like OGS or Vanier CGS) if eligible, though these often have earlier deadlines.

Application Checklist

Before submitting your application, ensure you have prepared the following components. If you need professional support in crafting a compelling statement of purpose or academic CV, CEE Writing Services can help you refine your application materials to meet Canadian university standards.

  • Check the specific graduate program deadline on the Carleton University Graduate Admissions website.
  • Secure a thesis supervisor (required by most PhD programs prior to application).
  • Prepare your Statement of Intent or Research Proposal.
  • Update your Academic CV highlighting research experience and publications.
  • Gather all official transcripts from previous post-secondary institutions.
  • Arrange for 2-3 academic reference letters (referees will be contacted directly).
  • Verify and meet English language proficiency requirements (IELTS, TOEFL, etc.).
  • Ensure all materials are submitted well ahead of the "guaranteed consideration" deadline for funding.
