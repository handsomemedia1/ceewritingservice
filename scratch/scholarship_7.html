The AfricaLics PhD Visiting Fellowship Programme (VFP) provides an exceptional opportunity for African PhD students working in the field of Innovation and Development to spend three months at the University of Johannesburg, South Africa. This fellowship is designed to support scholars by providing dedicated time, mentorship, and training to advance their doctoral research. It is important to note that this is a visiting fellowship for current PhD students, not a full PhD admission scholarship.

Last checked: October 2026

Current 2027 Application Status

The application cycle for the 2027 AfricaLics PhD Visiting Fellowship is currently open. The application deadline is October 2, 2026. The fellowship includes online pre- and post-activities starting in February 2027 and a physical residency at the University of Johannesburg scheduled from September 6 to December 3, 2027.

Funding Details

According to the official fellowship guidelines, the AfricaLics VFP provides comprehensive support for the 3-month residential study visit. The funding covers:

  • Travel: Return economy-class airfare from your nearest international airport to Johannesburg.
  • Accommodation: Fully covered for the duration of the 3-month stay.
  • Stipend: A monthly stipend of approximately US$600 to cover living expenses and local travel in Johannesburg.
  • Training & Mentorship: Costs for participation in joint courses and mentorship fees.
  • Insurance: Travel insurance (serving as basic health insurance) is provided.
  • Additional Support: Limited funds are available for participation in relevant short courses not available in the student's home country.

CEE recommends: Remember that this fellowship does not fund your basic PhD tuition or research costs at your home institution. You are expected to have secured your primary PhD funding from other sources. Treat this stipend as living support during your 3-month stay in South Africa, and ensure you budget accordingly.

Eligibility and Official Requirements

According to the university and AfricaLics guidelines, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Be currently enrolled as a PhD student at an African university.
  • Have a research focus related to Innovation and Development (I&D).
  • Be a citizen of an African country classified by the OECD/DAC as low-income or lower-middle-income.
  • Have completed your first year of PhD studies by December 2026.

Female students and candidates from low-income countries are highly encouraged to apply.

Application Checklist

To prepare a strong application, ensure you have the following documents ready before the deadline:

  • A brief outline of your current PhD project (including research objectives, methodology, and progress).
  • A motivation letter explaining why the AfricaLics Visiting Fellowship will benefit your PhD research and career in Innovation and Development.
  • Your updated Curriculum Vitae (CV).
  • A letter of support from your primary PhD supervisor at your home university.
  • Proof of current PhD registration.
