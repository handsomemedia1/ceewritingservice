Imperial College London offers one of the most prestigious funding opportunities for international students: the President's PhD Scholarships. This guide breaks down everything you need to know to submit a strong application for the 2027 entry cycle, tailored specifically for high-achieving applicants from Africa and around the world.

Last checked: October 2026

Current 2027 Application Status

Applications for the 2026/2027 cycle (for a 2027 start) are currently Open. The selection process takes place over three rounds throughout the academic year. It is highly recommended to apply as early as possible, as supervisors are limited to supporting only one applicant per cycle.

Detailed Funding Breakdown

When applying for this scholarship, it is important to understand exactly what is covered. This is a comprehensive package:

  • Full Tuition Fees: Your overseas tuition fees will be fully covered for up to 3.5 years.
  • Living Stipend: An annual tax-free living stipend at the UKRI rate plus a London weighting (typically enough to comfortably cover housing and living expenses in London) for up to 3.5 years.
  • Consumables Fund: An allowance of £2,000 per year for the first 3 years to cover research materials, equipment, and conference travel.

Official Requirements vs. Expert Advice

To maximize your chances, you must balance the university's baseline requirements with what actually makes an applicant stand out.

Academic Excellence

  • According to the university: You must have, or be on track to receive, a First Class UK undergraduate degree (or equivalent). If you have a Master's, it must be awarded with a Distinction.
  • CEE recommends: For international candidates, especially from Nigeria and other African countries, your transcript should clearly demonstrate top percentile performance. Emphasize any awards, publications, or significant research projects in your CV to contextualize your academic excellence globally.

Supervisory Support

  • According to the university: You must have contacted a supervisor who has agreed to support your application before you apply.
  • CEE recommends: Supervisors are highly selective because they can only support one candidate per cycle. Do not send generic emails. Read their recent publications and draft a highly tailored research proposal summary when reaching out. If you need help refining your academic outreach, consider utilizing CEE Writing Services to polish your proposal and introductory emails.

Application Checklist

Ensure you have completed all of the following steps before submitting your application:

  • Identify a potential academic supervisor at Imperial College London.
  • Reach out and secure their formal agreement to support your application.
  • Prepare your Statement of Purpose (Personal Statement) detailing your research interests and motivation.
  • Finalize a concise, structured research proposal (in collaboration with your prospective supervisor).
  • Gather all academic transcripts and degree certificates.
  • Select two strong academic referees (one must be your current or most recent academic supervisor).
  • Submit the standard Imperial College London PhD application online.
  • In the funding section of the application, explicitly select the option to be considered for the President's PhD Scholarships.

Applying for the President's PhD Scholarships requires significant preparation, but the reward is one of the most generous funding packages in the UK. Start early, communicate effectively with potential supervisors, and present a flawless application.
