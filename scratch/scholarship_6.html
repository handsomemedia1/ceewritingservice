The Commonwealth PhD Scholarship is one of the most prestigious fully funded opportunities for students from eligible Commonwealth countries to pursue doctoral studies in the United Kingdom. Designed to support future innovators, leaders, and researchers, this scholarship empowers individuals to contribute to the development of their home countries while gaining world-class academic experience.

Funding Details

The Commonwealth PhD Scholarship is exceptionally generous and covers all essential expenses for your doctoral study. According to the official provisions, the scholarship includes:

  • Full Tuition Fees: Complete coverage of all approved university tuition fees.
  • Living Stipend: A regular monthly living allowance to cover accommodation and daily expenses.
  • Travel Costs: Approved airfare from your home country to the UK at the start of your award, and a return flight at the end.
  • Additional Allowances: A warm clothing allowance upon arrival, a study travel grant towards the cost of study-related travel within the UK or overseas, and, in some cases, family allowances for eligible scholars.

Eligibility and Application Requirements

To be considered for the Commonwealth PhD Scholarship, applicants must meet several strict criteria. According to the CSC, you must:

  • Be a citizen of or have been granted refugee status by an eligible Commonwealth country.
  • Be permanently resident in an eligible Commonwealth country.
  • Be available to start your academic studies in the UK by September/October 2027.
  • Hold a first degree of at least upper second-class (2:1) honours standard, or a second-class degree and a relevant postgraduate qualification (usually a Master’s degree).

CEE recommends: Beyond the basic requirements, your research proposal is the most critical part of your application. The CSC focuses heavily on the development impact of your research. Ensure your proposal clearly demonstrates how your work will benefit your home country. Application Checklist

Ensure you have the following documents ready before submitting your application to the CSC portal and your national nominating agency:

  • Proof of citizenship or refugee status (e.g., passport).
  • Full academic transcripts and degree certificates (undergraduate and postgraduate).
  • At least two compelling letters of recommendation.
  • A well-structured research proposal demonstrating development impact.
  • Supporting statement from a proposed supervisor at a UK university (if required by your scheme).

Please note that many national nominating agencies have their own internal deadlines that fall significantly earlier than the CSC deadline of 20 October 2026. Always verify the specific internal deadline with your nominating body.
