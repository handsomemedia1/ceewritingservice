The University of Cambridge offers some of the most prestigious fully funded opportunities for international students to pursue a PhD. The two primary funding routes for Nigerian, African, and international applicants are the Gates Cambridge Scholarship and the Cambridge Trust awards. Navigating these applications requires careful planning, as the funding deadlines are strict and highly competitive.

Funding Details

According to the university, both the Gates Cambridge Scholarship and the Cambridge Trust awards are comprehensive. The funding typically includes:

  • University Composition Fee: Full coverage of tuition fees at the appropriate rate.
  • Maintenance Allowance: A generous stipend for a single student to cover living costs.
  • Travel Costs: One economy single airfare at both the beginning and end of the course.
  • Visa Costs: Inbound visa costs and the cost of the Immigration Health Surcharge.

CEE recommends factoring in additional personal expenses, as the stipend is designed for a single student. If you plan to bring dependents, you will need to demonstrate additional independent funding.

Application Advice and Requirements

According to the university, applicants must demonstrate outstanding intellectual ability, reasons for the choice of course, and a commitment to improving the lives of others (specifically for Gates Cambridge). You must also secure a potential supervisor before applying.

CEE recommends contacting potential supervisors at least two to three months before the funding deadline. Application Checklist

  • Identify a potential supervisor and secure their initial support.
  • Draft and refine your research proposal.
  • Prepare your personal statement (and the Gates Cambridge reference, if applicable).
  • Request academic and personal references early.
  • Complete the University of Cambridge Graduate Application Portal.
  • Submit by the course funding deadline (typically 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027 for international applicants).
